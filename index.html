ABOUT

Research at the environment–health interface

My work combines disease ecology, vector biology, geospatial analysis, and ecological modelling to investigate how environmental and climatic change can influence the potential distributions of vectors and the geography of infectious disease risk.

Biography & affiliations

I am an Associate Professor at Ain Shams University and Director of the One Health Unit at the Medical Ain Shams Research Institute (MASRI), Faculty of Medicine. My research focuses on One Health eco-epidemiology, vector-borne diseases, climate-sensitive health threats, and reproducible ecological modelling.

Research philosophy

Combine ecological understanding with transparent analytical workflows to support evidence-informed surveillance, research collaboration, and public-health preparedness.

  • Biologically informed modelling
  • Reproducible computational research
  • Clear communication of uncertainty
  • One Health collaboration
